About Our Trust

Why We Were Formed

Samaj Kalyan Trust was established in 2015 by a group of dedicated social workers from Pune who witnessed the challenges faced by rural students, neglected senior citizens, and the deteriorating environment. Driven by a shared vision of inclusive community development, they founded this trust to create lasting positive change in society.

Our Mission

To empower underprivileged communities through quality education, environmental conservation, and compassionate care for senior citizens, fostering self-reliance and dignity for all.

Our Vision

A society where every child has access to education, every elder lives with dignity, and our environment thrives for future generations.

President's Message

"Since our inception, we have believed that true service lies in empowering communities to stand on their own. Every uniform we distribute, every tree we plant, and every elder we comfort is a step towards the inclusive Maharashtra we dream of. I invite you to join us on this journey of compassion and change."

— Shri. Suresh Kulkarni

Rural School Education Support

📅 15 January 2026📍 Mulshi Taluka, Pune District👥 350+ Beneficiaries

In January 2026, Samaj Kalyan Trust organized a comprehensive education support drive at three Zilla Parishad schools in Mulshi Taluka. Our team of 40 volunteers worked tirelessly to ensure that every student received quality educational supplies.

Why This Activity Was Organized

Many students in rural areas of Pune district come from farming families with limited income. The cost of school uniforms, notebooks and stationery becomes a significant burden, often leading to school dropouts. Our trust identified these schools through community surveys and decided to provide comprehensive support.

Support Provided

  • 350 complete school uniform sets
  • 700 notebooks and writing pads
  • Geometry boxes, pencils, erasers and pens
  • School bags for 200 students
  • Motivational session with teachers and parents
"When the children received new uniforms, their eyes lit up. Some of them had never owned a new uniform before. This is the kind of impact that keeps us motivated." — Volunteer Priya Sharma

Tree Plantation Drive

📅 5 June 2026📍 Bhor, Pune District👥 200+ Volunteers

On World Environment Day 2026, our trust organized a large-scale tree plantation drive near Bhor in collaboration with the Forest Department and local panchayats. Over 200 volunteers from Pune joined hands to plant 2,000+ saplings of native species including Neem, Peepal, Banyan and Mango.

Impact

The planted saplings will collectively absorb an estimated 40 tonnes of CO₂ annually once fully grown. Local farmers have agreed to maintain the saplings, and our trust conducts quarterly survival audits.
